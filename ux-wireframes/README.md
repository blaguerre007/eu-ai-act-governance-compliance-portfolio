
AI Transparency & Human-Centered UX Wireframes

Author: Bergson C. Laguerre

Overview

This directory documents the user-facing AI
transparency concepts described in Section 5
of the Multi-Tier Regulatory Compliance Dossier.

The wireframes address AI interaction disclosures
under Article 50(1) of the EU AI Act.

Wireframe 1: Candidate Intake Disclosure

An AI-assisted recruitment interface designed
to inform applicants about:

- Interaction with an AI assistant
- Automated CV processing and skills matching
- Human hiring manager involvement
- Transparency and data processing information
- Available human review and assistance pathways

Wireframe 2: Conversational AI Interface

A persistent chatbot transparency interface
featuring:

- AI assistant identification
- An initial AI interaction notice
- A persistent AI indicator
- Audit and quality assurance messaging
- Access to transparency information
- A human agent request option

Design Principles

- Immediate AI disclosure
- Clear and understandable language
- Contextual transparency
- Accessible human assistance
- Human-centered governance

Purpose

Translate regulatory transparency requirements
into practical UX/UI design concepts for
recruitment and customer support applications.

These wireframes represent proposed designs
rather than validated production interfaces.
