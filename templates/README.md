
 AI Governance & Regulatory Compliance Templates

Author: Bergson C. Laguerre

 Overview

This directory contains reusable governance
templates derived from the regulatory and
technical controls in the Multi-Tier
Regulatory Compliance Dossier.

 Planned Templates

 1. AI Risk Assessment Matrix
Documents identified AI risks, detection
methods, mitigations, and review outcomes.

 2. EU AI Act Classification Matrix
Maps system functions to applicable
regulatory classifications and provisions.

 3. Technical Conformity Checklist
Covers requirements associated with
Articles 9-15 of the EU AI Act.

 4. Copyright Compliance Checklist
Supports Article 53 copyright policy
and training data transparency assessments.

 5. Human Oversight Checklist
Documents human review, intervention,
and approval requirements.

 6. Audit Logging Template
Defines proposed logging fields,
traceability requirements, and
human operator actions.

 7. Post-Market Monitoring Checklist
Supports operational monitoring,
incident assessment, and reporting.

 Purpose

Provide practical documentation structures
for assessing, recording, and reviewing
enterprise AI governance requirements.

Templates are illustrative and must be
adapted to the applicable legal obligations
and system implementation.
