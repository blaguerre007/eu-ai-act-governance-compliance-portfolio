
AI Governance & Regulatory Compliance Documentation

Author: Bergson C. Laguerre

Overview

This directory contains the regulatory assessments,
technical governance documentation, and compliance
artifacts developed for the EU AI Act Compliance &
Responsible AI Governance portfolio.

The project examines an enterprise SaaS platform
integrating general-purpose conversational AI into
two operational areas:

1. AI-assisted recruitment and candidate evaluation.
2. Customer support and sentiment analysis.

Documentation Sections

1. Executive Summary
Business context, AI system architecture, and
governance objectives.

2. EU AI Act Classification
Assessment of GPAI model obligations, high-risk
recruitment systems, and transparency requirements.

3. Regulatory Framework Mapping
Alignment between EU AI Act requirements,
NIST AI RMF, OWASP GenAI security controls,
and responsible AI principles.

4. Copyright and Data Transparency
Copyright compliance, data provenance,
and training data disclosure requirements.

5. Technical Conformity
Risk management, data governance, technical
documentation, logging, and human oversight.

6. AI Guardrails Architecture
Proposed input, dialog, retrieval, output,
and execution safety controls.

7. Audit and Monitoring
Audit trails, incident management,
and post-market monitoring procedures.

8. Governance Roadmap
Implementation milestones, verification,
and governance readiness activities.

Project Scope

This portfolio documents a regulatory assessment
and proposed governance architecture.

It does not represent a certified production
deployment or a formal legal compliance opinion.
